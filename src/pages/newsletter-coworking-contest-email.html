🏆 Coworking Contest Launch

Help Us Find the Most Beautiful Coworking Spaces

From day one of our magazine's existence, we've dreamed of creating a contest to celebrate the coworking spaces that fuel our productivity and creativity every day. There are so many incredible spaces out there—and we're fortunate to have beautiful locations hosting our magazine.

While many criteria matter—community, internet, location—few celebrate what we believe is equally important: design and beauty. Sometimes a simple table and chair suffice, but we want to put on a pedestal the creativity and thoughtfulness that owners and designers pour into spaces with remote workers' comfort in mind.

Since partnering with coworking spaces to distribute our magazine, we've been blown away by how many amazingly beautiful spaces exist worldwide. Our goal, with your help, is to find and celebrate the most beautifully designed coworking spaces serving remote workers and digital nomads every day.

How it works: The best spaces will be selected through public votes, with curation by the Nomad Magazine team and partners serving as jury. In print, we'll also share short stories of the selected winners.

Have ideas on how we can make this even better? Let us know!
