## Meet the Route

A full ship of **300+ digital nomads and entrepreneurs** will sail from neon-soaked Hong Kong 🇭🇰 to ramen-fueled cities in Japan 🇯🇵, pause for a pinch-me day on South Korea’s Jeju Island 🇰🇷, and wrap things up with a celebratory finale in the Philippines 🇵🇭.

- **Sat, Feb 14** – Set sail from Hong Kong  
- **Sun, Feb 15** – At sea (settle in, find your people)  
- **Mon, Feb 16** – At sea  
- **Tue, Feb 17** – Fukuoka, Japan  
- **Wed, Feb 18** – Nagasaki, Japan  
- **Thu, Feb 19** – Yatsushiro, Japan  
- **Fri, Feb 20** – Seogwipo, Jeju Island, South Korea  
- **Sat, Feb 21** – At sea  
- **Sun, Feb 22** – At sea  
- **Mon, Feb 23** – Hong Kong reunion day  
- **Tue, Feb 24** – At sea  
- **Wed, Feb 25** – Manila arrival (and the celebrations continue on land)
