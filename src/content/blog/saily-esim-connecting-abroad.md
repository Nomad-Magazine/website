## Step-by-Step Guide: Setting Up and Using Saily eSIM

Ready to set up your Saily eSIM? Whether you're gearing up for your first international adventure or you're a seasoned globetrotter, this guide will take you through the process - from purchase to troubleshooting.

### How to Purchase and Install Saily eSIM

Start by downloading the Saily app from either the [App Store](https://www.apple.com/app-store/) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/games?hl=en_US&gl=US). Once installed, create an account and explore the available plans tailored to your travel destination.

Buying a plan is straightforward. Pick a data package that suits your travel needs, and complete your purchase through the app. Payments are made in US dollars, and you can use major credit cards or digital payment options. After your payment is processed, you'll receive a QR code or detailed instructions to install your eSIM.

To install the eSIM, make sure your device has a stable internet connection and is both eSIM-compatible and unlocked for use with foreign networks [\[16\]](https://esim.holafly.com/reviews/saily-esim-not-working)[\[17\]](https://esim.holafly.com/reviews/saily-ultra-not-working). If your device's eSIM slots are full, remove any unused profiles before proceeding.

Simply scan the QR code provided or follow the app's step-by-step instructions to complete the installation. Avoid reinstalling an active eSIM, as this can cause issues. Once installed, activate your Saily eSIM and turn on data roaming. In most cases, the eSIM will activate automatically when you reach your destination, but you can also activate it manually through the app if needed.

From here, it’s a good idea to manage your data usage wisely to avoid running out unexpectedly.

### Tips for Managing Data Usage and Topping Up

Keeping track of your data usage is key to staying within your plan while traveling. Mobile data consumption often increases on trips, especially when Wi-Fi access is limited [\[13\]](https://saily.com/data-usage-calculator).

You can monitor your data usage directly in the Saily app or through your device's settings. Within the app, your remaining data balance is displayed clearly under the Saily eSIM section [\[12\]](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12823154589724-How-can-I-check-my-current-Saily-data-usage). You’ll also find information about your plan’s expiration date and balance [\[15\]](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/16518272109212-Can-I-check-my-Saily-eSIM-balance).

To save data, use Wi-Fi for tasks like downloading maps, playlists, or shows before your trip so you can access them offline [\[13\]](https://saily.com/data-usage-calculator). On iOS, disabling "Background App Refresh" can help conserve data, while Android users can enable data saver settings [\[13\]](https://saily.com/data-usage-calculator).

If you need more data, topping up is easy. Open the Saily app, select your eSIM, and choose the "add data" option. Complete the purchase, and your new plan will activate as soon as you arrive in a new country - provided your Saily eSIM is already active [\[14\]](https://support.saily.com/hc/en-us/articles/12823150321052-What-happens-when-I-run-out-of-data-on-my-eSIM).

Also, don’t forget to turn off data roaming for your primary physical SIM to avoid unexpected charges. This ensures all international data usage is routed through your Saily eSIM.

### Support and Troubleshooting for Travelers

Occasionally, you might run into connectivity issues. If that happens, here’s how to troubleshoot:

-   Make sure your Saily eSIM plan is active and data roaming is enabled in your device settings.
-   Turn off Wi-Fi to prioritize the eSIM's mobile data connection.
-   Restart your phone or toggle Airplane Mode on and off (wait about 15 seconds) to reset your network settings.
-   Check the Saily app to confirm you have enough data. Keep in mind that Saily Ultra subscribers have a 30GB high-speed data cap, after which speeds may slow down [\[17\]](https://esim.holafly.com/reviews/saily-ultra-not-working).

If connectivity is still an issue, manually search for available networks on your device and try connecting to a different operator. Double-check that your destination is included in your Saily plan [\[17\]](https://esim.holafly.com/reviews/saily-ultra-not-working).

For Android users, ensure the eSIM is set as the preferred mobile data option and disconnect from Wi-Fi. If all else fails, resetting your device’s network settings can help, but you’ll need to reconfigure data roaming and APN settings afterward [\[16\]](https://esim.holafly.com/reviews/saily-esim-not-working).

Still stuck? Saily's customer support team is available through the app and website. Be prepared to provide details like your device model, location, and any error messages to help them resolve your issue quickly.
