NordVPN protects your data on public Wi-Fi by using advanced encryption to establish a private "tunnel" between your device and its servers. On top of that, NordVPN conceals your IP address, making it harder for websites or third parties to track your location or identify your device. With these safeguards, you can use public Wi-Fi confidently, knowing your privacy and security are well-protected.

### How can I set up NordVPN on my devices before traveling?
