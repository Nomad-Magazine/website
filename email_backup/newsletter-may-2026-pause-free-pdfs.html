2. A pause in print ... for now

Now the harder part. We are taking a break from publishing new print editions for some time, at least through the end of this year. After that we will decide whether a new edition makes sense (we reserve the right to change our minds at any time. 😁)

Creating four beautiful print editions with my team has been an amazing creative journey. It was my life, my friends, my connections and adventures, my MBA, and my proof that “everything is possible” all at once. Thank you to everyone who believed in us and supported Nomad Magazine over the last two years (and longer). It has been fun, and I hope we get to do more of it someday.

The reason is simple: as founder and main investor in this project, I need to focus on other family businesses that are more profitable.

We are not going quiet online. We will keep publishing articles on nomad-magazine.com and on our new Substack during the pause, with Jessica Holmes, our senior writer and editor, leading the way. These are hand-written and edited articles based on personal experience.

How you can support us: download counts will play a real role in whether we continue publishing. If you want to help, share the official download links with your friends and communities, please do not email PDF files around; send the links instead.
