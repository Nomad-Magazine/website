Finally, Mobile Reading That Feels Right

Nomad Magazine × ZINIO Partnership

You asked, we listened. You've been requesting a better digital reading experience beyond flipbooks and PDFs. We heard you loud and clear.

Digital development has been a challenge for us. We've explored various service providers, some charging upwards of €700 per month (we hope to afford premium solutions like these in the future). But we believe in finding smart solutions that work for our community now. When you look hard enough, solutions come your way.

We're excited to announce our partnership with ZINIO, a leading digital magazine platform and distributor trusted by millions of readers worldwide. ZINIO offers a premium app with rich functionality designed specifically for an exceptional magazine reading experience.

📱 Read Nomad Magazine Anywhere

  • Interactive Features: Tap on links, zoom into images
  • Customizable Reading: Adjust text size, colors, and background
  • Beautiful Layout: Optimized for every screen size
  • Cloud Sync: Start on mobile, continue on desktop
  • Library Access: All your issues in one place
